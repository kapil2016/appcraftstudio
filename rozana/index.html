Free ways to stay safe

  • Backup file: in the app, open Backup → Make backup file and send it to yourself on WhatsApp or save it in Drive. On a new phone, choose Load from backup file. It holds what was there when you made it, so make one often.
  • Android backup: if Google backup is on in your phone's settings, Android may keep a copy and restore it when you reinstall. It is not guaranteed — don't rely on it alone.
