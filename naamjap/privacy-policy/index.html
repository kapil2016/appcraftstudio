Naam Jap Privacy Policy

Naam Jap for Android (com.jaapcounter) · by appcraftstudio · Effective 28 October 2026

In short
  • Naam Jap has no accounts and no servers. Your counts, malas, history and settings stay on your phone.
  • The free version shows one Google AdMob ad when the app opens, at most once an hour and never while you chant. Google collects some device data to show these ads.
  • Removing ads is a Google Play purchase. Google Play handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following only on your device, in its private storage. None of it is sent to us:

  • Your chant counts, completed malas, daily history and streak.
  • Your chosen mantra, mala size and other settings.
  • Whether you have bought ad-free, when you first opened the app and when an ad was last shown, so the app can keep to the ad limits below.

This data is not included in Android’s cloud backup. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes it. You can also clear today’s count in the app’s Settings.

Advertising

The free version shows one full-screen ad from Google AdMob when you open the app, or when you come back to it after a while. It never shows an ad during your first day, more than once an hour, over a menu, or once you have started chanting.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and the approximate location it indicates;
  • information about your interactions with ads, and diagnostic information about the ad software.

Google’s use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. The app asks Google only for ads suitable for general audiences.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings, usually under Google → Ads or Privacy → Ads.

Purchases

You can remove ads with a monthly subscription or a one-time purchase. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play under Google’s terms; we never see your card, UPI or other payment details. The app asks Google Play which of its products your Google account owns, so it knows whether to show ads. You can cancel the subscription at any time in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions.

The app can also count with your phone’s volume buttons while it is open. This needs no permission, and you can turn it off in the app’s Settings.

Children

Naam Jap is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Security and retention

We don’t collect or keep personal data on our side. Connections to Google’s ad and Play services are encrypted in transit. Data stored on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Naam Jap handles data, we will update this page and its effective date.
