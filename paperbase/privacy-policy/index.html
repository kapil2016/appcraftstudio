Paperbase Privacy Policy

Paperbase for Android (com.paperbase.app) · by appcraftstudio · Effective 25 October 2026

In short
  • Your scanned papers, their fields and their text are stored on your phone. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • When Read papers with AI is on and you are online, a scanned page image is sent to be read by Google’s Gemini AI. We do not keep the image or its text. You can switch this off in the app’s Vault.
  • To limit AI reading per phone, our server keeps a random app ID with a daily count of pages read — nothing else.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Paperbase is made by appcraftstudio (“we”, “us”). If you have a question about this policy or your data, email craftconsole88@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you scan is stored in Paperbase’s private storage on your phone: page images, the title, fields and full text read from each paper, your drawers and your settings. Searching happens on the phone. We have no copy of any of it.

  • Camera. Paperbase does not ask for camera permission. To scan, it opens Google’s document scanner (part of Google Play services on Android), or your phone’s camera app, which hands the cropped page back to Paperbase.
  • Photos. Paperbase has no access to your photo gallery. When you add images from the gallery, Android’s photo picker gives Paperbase only the images you choose.
  • Reading on the phone. Every scan is first read on the phone with Google ML Kit text recognition, which runs on the device.
  • Sharing. A paper or a spreadsheet export leaves the app only when you share it yourself, through Android’s share sheet, to the place you pick.
  • Android backup. If you have Android’s own backup turned on, Android may include Paperbase’s database and settings in your Google account backup, so a new phone can restore them. Page images are not included. This is controlled by your phone’s backup settings, not by us.

Uninstalling Paperbase deletes everything it stored on your phone.

What leaves your phone, and why

1. Reading papers with AI

For better fields and a full transcription, Paperbase can have each scanned page read by AI. This is on by default and can be switched off at any time in the app (Vault → Read papers with AI). When it is on and the phone is online, the page images of a paper are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to the Paperbase server, together with the names of the fields that drawer already uses. The server passes them to Google’s Gemini API, which returns a title, the key/value fields and the text of the page. The result is saved on your phone.

The Paperbase server does not store page images or the text read from them; it only passes them through. If a read fails, the server may log Google’s error message (never the image or its content) for troubleshooting. Google processes the images to provide this service under the Gemini API Terms of Service. Papers scanned while AI reading is off never leave your phone.

2. The daily AI allowance

So that one phone cannot use up the service, each phone may have a limited number of pages read by AI per day. For this the app creates a random app ID when it is first opened. It is not derived from your phone’s hardware identifiers and is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account. Our server stores, against that app ID, the date and how many pages were read with AI that day. Nothing about the content of your papers is stored.

3. Cloud features

Cloud search across phones and backup to Google Drive are not active in this version of Paperbase; the app shows them as “coming soon”. If we turn them on, they will be optional, they will require you to sign in with Google, and we will update this policy before they are available.

We do not sell, rent or trade your data, and we do not use it for advertising. Data is handled only by the service providers that make the features work:

How long we keep it

Page images and the text read from them are not stored on our server. Daily page counts are small records with no content; we keep them only as long as needed to run the daily allowance and to understand overall use, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off AI reading. In the Vault, switch off “Read papers with AI”. Paperbase keeps working fully, reading papers on the phone only, and nothing is sent off the phone.
  • Delete your papers. Remove papers or drawers in the app, or uninstall it.
  • Delete server records. Email craftconsole88@gmail.com and we will delete the daily counts kept for your app ID within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, laws such as India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU GDPR give you further rights: to access, correct or erase your data, and to complain. Contact us at the address above to use any of them.

Security

Data sent between the app and our server is encrypted with HTTPS. The server keeps only the minimum described above. No system is perfectly secure, but your papers themselves never rest anywhere but your phone.

Children

Paperbase is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Where data is processed

Google and Cloudflare run servers in many countries, so AI reading may be processed outside your country. Paperbase’s server runs in Singapore. Both providers use industry-standard protections.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Paperbase handles data, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
