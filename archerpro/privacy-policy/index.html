1. Who we are

Archer Pro ("the game") is a stickman archery game for Android published on Google Play by appcraftstudio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game and the services built into it handle, and the choices you have.

2. What we collect

We do not collect, store or sell your personal information. The game has no sign-up, no accounts of its own, no analytics of ours, and it never sends your data to servers run by us (we don't have any).

The game does use Google services that process some data on their own terms, described below.

3. Data stored on your phone

Your game progress is saved in the game's private storage on your phone: levels and stars, coins, bows and upgrades, settings (sound, vibration) and a record of completed purchases so coins are never lost or added twice.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this save in your device backup. Google handles that backup under the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Google Play Games and cloud save

The game uses Google Play Games Services. When you are signed in to Play Games, the game saves the same progress listed above to the Saved Games area of your Play Games account, so you can get it back after reinstalling or on a new phone. Your Play Games profile name may be shown by Google when you sign in.

This data is stored by Google, not by us. Google Play Games Services may also collect diagnostic and usage data to run its service. You can delete the game's saved data from your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account. If you don't sign in, your progress stays only on your phone.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob:

  • Rewarded videos that you choose to watch, for the daily gift or a free revive.
  • Full-screen ads between levels now and then. These are never shown to players who have bought coins.

To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be worked out;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data such as crash and performance logs.

Google may use this data to show personalized ads where the law and your choices allow, and shares it with advertising partners as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your ad choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent with Google's consent message before any ad is loaded. You can change your choice at any time with AD PRIVACY in the game's Settings.
  • Everyone: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the phone).

6. In-app purchases

Coin packs are sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the payment; we never see or store your card or payment details. The game only receives a confirmation from Google Play that a purchase was completed (an order token), so it can add the coins. That confirmation is kept with your game progress as described above. Google's handling of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

8. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for ads, cloud save and purchases.
  • Billing: required by Google Play for in-app purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Vibration: for feedback during play; you can turn it off in Settings.

The game does not ask for your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

9. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it with the relevant Google service.

10. Keeping and deleting data

  • Progress on your phone: use RESET PROGRESS in Settings, or uninstall the game, to delete it.
  • Cloud save: delete the game's data from your Google Play Games profile (Play Games app → Profile → Settings → Delete Play Games account and data, or the game's entry there).
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings; Google keeps ad data under its own retention rules.

Because we hold no data about you on our side, there is nothing for us to delete, but we are happy to help with any request.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. For data handled by Google services, use the controls described above or Google's own tools. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

12. Security

Your save stays in the game's private storage on your phone. Data sent to Google services travels over encrypted connections and is protected by Google's security measures.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the new version of the game is released.
